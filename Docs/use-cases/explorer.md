# Explorer

**Persona:** Explorer  
**Benchmark:** [Stationary Satellite Relays](../design-benchmark.md) — speculative thought experiment that may harden into research

An explorer uses Ideate to **follow curiosity** without pretending the work is a paper or a product yet. Speculation stays tagged. Open questions are inventory, not failure.

## Job

Home asks: *What thought is growing, and what should we not pretend to know?*

Success: questions preserved; `object_category` distinguishes speculation, unknown, and later evidence-backed claims.

Example workspaces: *Could a satellite relay last 1,000 years?*; Purana as open inquiry (if the goal is wonder rather than a course, use Explorer; if the goal is understanding a text, use [Student](./student.md)).

## Lifecycle

A thought experiment is first-class ([vision.md](../vision.md)). It enters as a Thought, accumulates concepts and assumptions, branches into hypotheses, meets evidence or stays unknown, and may become a research proposition — or a valuable dead end.

| Step | Explorer |
| --- | --- |
| Think | What if the whole architecture were modular and dormant? |
| Explore | Walk implications without a deadline |
| Connect | Analogies, prior abandoned ideas |
| Question | What must we not claim? |
| Test | Thought experiment, Fermi estimate, later literature |
| Revise | New version of the speculation |
| Create | A clearer question or a promotion to Researcher / Inventor |

## Killer features

- **Unknown** cards (“I don’t know yet”) on the Concept → Hypothesis path.
- Epistemic tags on the card footer (speculation vs claim).
- **DEEP / BATCH** only when they ask to critically evaluate the whole theory — not every curious turn ([java-backend-ai.md](../java-backend-ai.md)).
- Failure-preserving: abandoned relay concepts remain queryable.

## Objects emphasized

Thought, Concept, Unknown, Question, Assumption, Hypothesis (when earned), Critique. Evidence appears when they actually look; it is not required to start.

## What this is not

- A toy “brainstorming canvas” with no versions
- A second-class project because there is no paper yet
