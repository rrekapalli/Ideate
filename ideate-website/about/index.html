About Ideate

An AI-native environment where ideas, knowledge, and understanding can be explored, connected, challenged, and continuously evolved. The original intention: an evolutionary system for human ideas.

The category error to avoid

The mistake would be to build “software for researchers.” Research tools optimize literature capture. Note tools optimize document capture. Tutors optimize curriculum completion. Chat assistants optimize the next answer. Ideate optimizes knowledge evolution.

Four layers

  1. Human thinker — student, researcher, inventor, professional.
  2. AI collaborator — teach, question, critique, research, connect, guide.
  3. Idea graph — concepts, questions, evidence, hypotheses, experiments, decisions.
  4. Evolution — version, history, growth, contradictions, discovery.

The graph is the data structure. AI is the reasoning layer. Versioning is the historical layer. Evidence is the epistemic layer. The workspace is where an idea grows.

Epistemic honesty

The system must not collapse these into one “AI answer”:

  • Established knowledge / fact
  • Supported inference
  • Hypothesis
  • Speculation / thought experiment
  • Open question / unknown
  • Misconception

A student should leave knowing what is established, what is inferred, what is theirs, and what is still unknown. That is epistemic literacy.

Why a thought experiment is first-class

A speculative question is not second-class because it lacks published evidence. It enters as a Thought, accumulates concepts, branches, meets evidence, and may become a research proposition or a dead end. The dead end remains valuable because it records how understanding evolved.
